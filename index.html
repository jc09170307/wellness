Ear Shen Men
Pinch the triangular fossa at the top of your ear. Firm. Breathe in through nose, out through mouth.

Ear Craving Point (tragus)
Pinch the small cartilage flap at your ear canal entrance. Both sides. The craving signal is interrupting now.
