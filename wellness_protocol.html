CYP3A4 Inhibitor: Schisandra is a clinically significant inhibitor of CYP3A4 enzymes. Your medications metabolized by CYP3A4 include:
• Aripiprazole (Abilify) — CYP3A4 substrate · Schisandra can raise aripiprazole blood levels unpredictably → increased side effects (sedation, EPS risk)

 • Tadalafil — partially CYP3A4 · interaction possible
 Clinical data (Medsafe NZ; Frontiers in Pharmacology 2022): "Schisandra extracts pose a significant risk for elevating blood levels of medicines that are CYP3A substrates."

Bottom line: Do NOT add Schisandra to your stack without explicit clearance from both Dr. Lau AND Dr. Lawson. The liver benefit is real, but the Abilify interaction makes this high-risk unsupervised. Good alternatives: Yin Chen Hao and Dan Shen provide liver support without this risk.
